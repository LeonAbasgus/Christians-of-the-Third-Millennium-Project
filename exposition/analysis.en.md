A systematic exposition and critical analysis of the three volumes of To Christians of the Third Millennium by Hierodeacon Panteleimon (Leon Adzhindzhal).

## Introduction

A person may know what to do and yet be unable to act accordingly. An institution can proclaim a person's independence and at the same time deprive him or her of the ability to object, make decisions and be held accountable for them. A believer may do the prescriptions without understanding how church life becomes his own answer to God. The project Christians of the Third Millennium explores the connection between these difficulties.

The subject of the project is the conditions of human authorship. A person receives existence, language, culture and capacities before being able to choose anything. Yet this received condition does not make every act a mere continuation of external influence. A response of one’s own is possible: a person discerns what is happening, initiates an action, accepts its consequences and allows for correction. CTM examines how such a response becomes possible, what impedes it and what forms of life together support its development.

In the Christian perspective, the capacity to respond has a received ground. The entire project should therefore be read in light of the primacy of God’s action. Human authorship does not first arise independently of God and then acquire a religious supplement. Grace is presupposed from the outset as the condition of possibility of freedom. The response made through that received capacity remains the person’s own. [1; 3, «В поисках пневматологии», §14]

The practical purpose of CTM is to study the valid basis for practices and institutions. The method allows you to ask what person or organization is able to see, what actions are available to them, who has the right to change a decision and who pays for it. It also provides for the design of conditions under which one's own response becomes feasible. Neither an analyst, a pastor, nor a leader can produce another's authorship for the person.

Three volumes reveal this problem from different angles. The first formulates the philosophical and theological question of human existence. The second describes the origin, quality and development of subjecthood. The third article develops the procedure for analyzing and designing subjects' architectures, while the final article addresses pneumatology and triadology. Their relationship is the subject of this review.

The review has the status of a secondary exposition. The links lead to the names of chapters and sections of the Russian corps; where paragraph numbering is used, the corresponding work is indicated. The author's explanations agreed for this review are marked separately and do not appear as quotes from volumes. The educational examples are illustrations of the review. Critical judgments are compiled in a separate section and not attributed to the author of the CTM.

## 1 The Starting Question and the Unity of the Three Volumes

The project begins with a functional question about the Church: what does church life make possible for man? This question requires that we determine what kind of person Christianity is, what freedom is and what it means to live by faith. Therefore, the analysis of church practice leads to a study of the basis for human action. [1, «Функционал Церкви»]

The original diagnosis of CTM is the loss of ontological initiative by Christianity. It is a situation in which Christian words, customs and belongings are preserved, but valid decisions are determined by other factors. For example, a professed trust in God may coexist with a practice where the highest criterion is to maintain status or avoid disapproval. This does not allow for a prejudgment of each believer or institution; the diagnosis sets the subject of study  the difference between the professed and the practiced. [1, введение к «Опыту прикладной метафизики субъекта»]

The first volume puts a person before the question of what he is made of. The second is how the resulting existence can become its own beginning. The third explores the distribution of opportunities for such a beginning between participants in specific relationships and institutions. Thus, personal spiritual work and social analysis are linked: the ability to respond depends on both a person's position and the circumstances under which he must act.

The name applied metaphysics expresses this connection. Metaphysical premises are explored in their actions: what opportunities they open, what they exclude, what makes them real and what answers they make imaginable. Philosophical work becomes practical when a person is able to discover his own precondition and test its consequences.

## 2 Reality and the Foundations of Perception

### Reality and the Map

The original distinction between CTM is the difference between reality and its human map. Reality precedes a specific act of its meaning and is not exhaustive by this meaning. The map is a way of orientation: it includes ideas about the existing, causal, meaningful, permissible and possible. The limitations of the map do not render it useless; they require correction. [1, «Опыт прикладной метафизики субъекта», гл. 1; 3, разд. II]

The working difference between ontology and metaphysics is expressed by the author in this formula: Onthology  what is real to me. Metaphysics  why I think it's real  In the methodological description, the current ontology also includes a device of perception and action: how something becomes significant, what connections are recognized and what responses are allowed.

Suppose the head of a school says that he values initiative. If the employee's proposals never change the decision and disagreement is punished, the actual organization of the relationship differs from that description. The CTM suggests investigating the discrepancy itself: what procedures and expectations reproduce it, who notices it, and whether there is a possibility of correction. Intentional lying is only one possible explanation; the usual order may seem natural to participants.

### Acknowledging the Truth

The first volume examines truth through a willingness to be based on the reality of discovery. Seeing a mistake and agreeing to change your actions because of it is different. Therefore, knowledge has an existential dimension: true judgment can demand recognition of one's own wrongdoing, renunciation of advantage or acceptance of responsibility. [1, «Опыт прикладной метафизики субъекта», гл. 1, §§5–6]

In this way, the willingness to accept the consequences of truth must be accompanied by a test of judgment. Honesty and determination do not prove it true. A person can courageously follow a false idea. So the distinction between reality and map applies to both Christian self-description and the conclusions of CTM analysts.

### Pre-reflective Axiomatics

In “The Genesis of Subjecthood,” the pre-reflective axiomatics of the position of subjecthood comprises four practical orientations that guide a person’s life before they can be formulated conceptually: the hermeneutics of beginning, the hermeneutics of responsibility, methodological monotheism and ontological verifiability. These involve accepting one’s received existence, assuming one’s own response, determining its ultimate orientation and allowing it to be tested. The pre-reflective here is neither false nor imposed by definition. It concerns the conditions under which a position of subjecthood takes shape. [2, «Генезис субъектности», §5]

Language, habits, body skills and institutions can cement this architecture even before humans are able to describe it. In ontological memory, the experience of cooperation and expectations arising from betrayal or disaster is preserved. This memory facilitates a stable operation, but it can narrow the horizon of what is visible and increase the price of correction. Hence, the inherited practice is capable of both developing a recognition of freedom and strengthening dependence. [3, §§43–46, 165–166]

The transmission of experience is also not limited to the transfer of ready explanations. The CTM methodology shows that example, joint practice, ascetic discipline and obedience can form the ability to distinguish and respond before a student is able to fully understand and express the basics of an action being proposed. Such transmission should lead to one's own responsibility, not dependence on an external decision maker. [3, §§164–166]

## 3 Grace and the Possibility of One’s Own Response

### The Gift of a Horizon

The "Interpretation Condition" of the first volume clearly states that in all human choice and ability to take a certain position, grace is presumed as a condition for the possibility of this choice. This instruction applies to all reasoning, not just theological chapters. [1, «Интерпретационное условие»]

In the author's clarification adopted for this presentation, grace is understood as a gift of the Trinity Personal God, a horizon in which freedom, humanity and one's own response are possible. The word "horizon" indicates a condition of possibility; it does not make God impersonal or replace theological teaching about grace. The personal is the Giver Himself. [1, «Интерпретационное условие»; 3, «В поисках пневматологии», §14; 4]

It is necessary to distinguish between a given ability and its implementation in a specific response. The interpretation condition  applies grace to all the provisions of choice; an article on pneumatology links its action with healing the ability to see, distinguish, desire and respond. In this regard, the recognition of choice should be understood as being within a given ability, not as man's creation of freedom out of nothing. [1, «Интерпретационное условие»; 3, «В поисках пневматологии», §14]

### Received Capacity and Authorship of the Act

God and man are not described here as two participants with limited scope of action. Nature healing can increase a person's ability to see, distinguish, desire and act. The acquisition of this ability does not make a specific human act fictitious. [3, «В поисках пневматологии», §14]

It does not mean that every deed is good. A person can use his real ability to answer for a false or destructive action. The distinction between the gift of ability and the content of an answer is necessary so that responsibility for evil does not fall on God.

Failure to acknowledge the gift gives no grounds for denying anyone human dignity. The author’s clarification concerns the risk of losing attained personal subjecthood, the capacity to discern, enact and own one’s response. This distinction precludes using the method to rank people by their right to be considered human. [4]

### Grace and the Limits of Verification

In the article In research on pneumatology , a boundary is drawn between an analysis of human architecture and a statement about God's specific action. A high level of competence, the ability to correct one's own map or take initiative cannot be used to prove a gracious truth. The absence of such evidence does not prove the absence of grace, but it prohibits considering the connection to be analytically established. [3, «В поисках пневматологии», §17]

Thus, grace is the original theological basis for the possibility of an answer, and the method explores the claims, actions, and conditions available to human judgment. These levels are related, but they're not interchangeable.

## 4 Subjecthood and the Limit of Autonomy

### Three Meanings of Dependence

A man doesn't start with himself. His body, language, history and attitude were given before his choice. This is not a defect that needs to be addressed. The question arises when the conditions obtained exhaust the action so much that a person only reproduces the ready-made installation and does not take his own position in relation to it. [2, «Генезис субъектности»]

For reading the body, it's useful to distinguish between existence, lack of own authorship and non-sovereign authorship. In the third case, man answers himself, though not being the source of his own existence. For example, accepting someone else's advice may be your own decision; inventing an unusual act on your own, on the contrary, does not guarantee meaningful authorship.

The foundation marks the beginning of a subjective position: a person takes on the question of what he acts from and for. The decolonization of the individual is about the ways in which other people's institutions take their place as a final foundation. It doesn't require the destruction of all inherited connections. The inheritance can be recognized, verified and held accountable.

### The Radical Formulation in Volume I

In the experiment of applied metaphysics, autonomy is pushed to the limit. The text requires a real attempt to hold back the universal claim with its own means and affirms the inevitability of its failure. It is in the original Russian and is not an addition to the commentator. [1, «Опыт прикладной метафизики субъекта», гл. 2, §9]

The content of the extreme test is to discover that it's impossible to be a definitive ground for yourself. And yet, refusing to acknowledge our own inadequacies is destroying the conformity of reality. To maintain the subject's position is to recognize a limit without compensating for it with lies and not taking responsibility for your answer.

The second and third volumes describe the authorship within the resulting existence. Therefore, when reading together, it is necessary to separate the independence of the answer from the claim for self-sufficiency in life. The first is a liability condition; the second is a limitation. This distinction helps to connect the two volumes, but it does not in itself prove that every person has to go through the same biographical sequence of crises. The question of the universality of the first volume's requirements remains relevant for critical analysis.

*Critical discussion.* A determinist can object here. The fact that a person can weigh reasons, reconsider an intention and accept the consequences leaves open whether the entire process is exhausted by prior causes. CTM distinguishes the causal history of an act from the person’s relation to the conditions that shaped it. Explaining the origin of a belief does not by itself establish its truth. Likewise, knowing what caused an act does not by itself settle its authorship. This distinction makes it possible to study a person’s own response within given conditions. To answer the determinist, one must still show how the subject acts within those conditions and how that action relates to prior causation.

### Readdressing and the Cross

Ontological re-adressing means refusing to attribute to one's own ability to be the ultimate source of existence. In the Christian response, God-Logos is recognized as the Foundation. The life of the deceased is accepted without removing his name or responsibility. [1, «К онтологии Логоса»]

The cross reveals this relationship in a finite form. In the author's account, it is related to faithfulness to truth and humanity while rejecting self-preservation as a supreme criterion. It does not justify arbitrary suffering or make submission to human authority the equivalent of loyalty to God. In the text itself, the rejection of ontological autonomy is distinguished from a totalitarian dissolution of personality by preserving and strengthening responsibility.

The transition to Christ should be represented in his Christian identity. The philosophical discovery of the limits of self-reason and the confession of Jesus Christ are not one judgment. The review should show their connection, without giving a sequence of statements as the completed proof of each theological message.

### The Logos and the Ground of Relationship

In the first volume, Logos links the meaningful determination of Reality and the possibility of communication. Closed relationships make participants the last measure of each other. In a foundational relation, each can recognize the independence of the other's answer because both relate to a basis that neither has authority over. In the Christian religion, Logos is the Son of God. Methodological monotheism requires that ultimate attachments and obligations be linked to a single, finite Basis. Religious practices, people, or institutions can become idolatry when their present form is tested and made the final measure of truth. [1, «К онтологии Логоса»; 2, «Генезис субъектности», §5.3; 3, §150]

*Critical discussion.* The distinction between Reality and the map accounts for the possibility of correction. Experience can refute an expectation, and the consequences of an action can reveal a connection absent from the map. A map’s adequacy thus depends on what exceeds its current contents. The move to the Logos as a personal Ground requires further premises. One must show why revisable criteria applicable in particular domains are insufficient, why their coordination requires a single ultimate Ground, and what warrants understanding that Ground as personal. Methodological monotheism formulates the requirement of unity. Christian confession names that Ground as the Logos. Connecting these two steps calls for an argument in its own right.

It is equally important to distinguish the conditions of communication from explicit agreement about its ultimate Ground. People with different convictions can acknowledge one another’s right to respond, limit their own power and revise their shared rules. This experience raises a question about what gives such recognition binding force and how durable it is when the rules cease to benefit one party. CTM grounds the ultimate binding force of the relationship in the Logos. To justify this move, its account must be tested against what mutual self-restraint and revisable shared norms can explain. A shared confession of the Ground must also be tested in practice: can the other person object, refuse or give an unexpected response and still be heard?

### The Image of God and the Fullness of the Person

The theme of God's image links human dignity to the gift of a personal answer. The analogy points to the human condition in relation to God. The difference between dignity and subjecthood allows us to talk about the growth or loss of ability to respond, while maintaining recognition of each person's dignity. In the face of worship, full communication with God preserves a personal irreplaceable and distinctive nature between the Creator and creation. [1, «К метафизике личности»; 2, «Эсхатологическая субъектность»; 4]

### Incarnation, Cross and Resurrection

In the incarnation, the divine initiative is part of human history. In the Passions Christ allows human judgment to take place and on the Cross accepts the valid consequences of an unjust sentence. The seriousness of human freedom is revealed even in a false answer. Christ's acceptance of this price opens the door to responsibility for one's conduct and preserves another's freedom. The resurrection opens up a fulness that human endurance and historical success cannot produce. These events set the Christian horizon for understanding human subjecthood. [2, «Эсхатологическая субъектность»; 3, §14]

## 5 Action and Personal Eschatology

Ontic action is carried out within a given order: solves a specific task, reproduces skill or maintains an existing life organization. The ontological question is about the very order of what basis and possibilities it implies. CTM is exploring the transitions that change how people relate to reality. [1, «Опыт прикладной метафизики субъекта», гл. 3; 2, «Онтогенез субъектности»]

Volume I uses the radical language of rupture and contrasts ontological action with the entropic reproduction of the existing order. The original explicitly states that ontic action reproduces distortion. In the context of the corpus as a whole, this must be read alongside the discussion of crystallization, stable competencies and institutions in Volumes II and III. Education, treatment and maintenance of infrastructure can enable one’s own response. Stability and repetition alone do not indicate loss of subjecthood.

The ontological stop indicates the limit of the previous mode of action. It does not guarantee transformation: it is possible to deny inconsistency, restore an old pattern or find a new answer. Generative transition means the emergence of a different architecture of possibilities, and crystallization  the consolidation of an emerging ability in a reproducible form. This is a necessary consolidation so that the new beginning does not remain a single episode. [2, «Онтогенез субъектности»; 3, разд. III]

Personal eschatology sets the boundary of this formation. Eschaton is not a predicted future or successful historical project. The attitude toward the future Kingdom changes the measure of the present: neither government, organization, nor professional role can exhaust the purpose of a person. [1, «Церковь как инфраструктура формирования персональной эсхатологии»; 2, «Эсхатологическая субъектность»]

Faith as a competence means the ability to respond steadily in uncertainty, risk and lack of ready instruction. It involves discernment, learning and the ability to correct. Fear or lack of quick success doesn't automatically refute it. At the same time, religious conviction does not free a person from examining facts and consequences of his actions. [1, «Вера как компетенция»]

### Historical Openness and the Fullness of the Eschaton

The eschatological horizon allows us to assess historical forms, act within them and re-examine them in the face of reality. Its meta-project function opens up the possibility of various historical projects. None of them have the right to give the ultimate Kingdom. In the proper Christian sense, eschaton includes the resurrection of the body, the transformation of creation, God's Kingdom and worship. Eternal ontological communication means the involvement of God's life with preserving the personality and distinctions between the Creator and creation. [2, «Эсхатологическая субъектность»]

### God’s Language, Faith and Repentance

The language of God asks how the confession of a God of love relates to suffering and experiencing His silence. Competitive ontology tends to see God as a supreme force designed to ensure victory for its side. Solidarity ontology asks the question of a relationship that preserves the freedom of another. This distinction changes the understanding of divine action and leaves open the question of what specific suffering is. Repentance and ontological asceticism affect the basis from which a person sees what is happening, evaluates himself and responds. [1, «Язык Бога», «Вера как компетенция»; 2, «Качество онтологии», §6.2]

## 6 The Architecture of Response

### Dynamic Iconic Space and Operating System

Dynamic Iconic Space, or DIS, describes the configuration of what is present to a person as real and meaningful. It includes images, connections, scenes, expectations, ideas about ourselves and others, time and the Foundation. The question is not just the amount of information, but what kind of relationships a person can maintain at the same time. [2, «Качество онтологии»; 3, разд. II]

The operating system, or OS, describes the ways in which a recognized object works. Its receptive-interpretational contours are related to perception and understanding, the projective-executive  with constructing and implementing action, the reflective-correctional  with learning on detected inconsistencies. These contours are not individual subjects: the whole person sees, decides and acts.

Subjecthood in this architecture refers to one's own beginning and responsibility for it. In the analysis of institutions, the powers to recognize, interpret, initiate, participate, object, correct and stop action are essential. The formal position does not always coincide with the actual distribution of these opportunities. [3, разд. VIII]

The formula DIS × OS × subjecthood denotes the interdependence of measurements. It doesn't suggest that you calculate identity by multiplying three numbers. A broad vision without a way of doing things can remain fruitless; technical competence with a narrow map  serve an erroneous purpose; formal freedom without means of implementation  remains a declaration.

### Quality of Ontology

The quality of ontology is multifaceted. They have the importance of horizon width, connectivity of differences, ability to withstand contradictions, ways of acting, openness to correction and relationship to the Fundamentals. Efficiency in achieving a goal is only one aspect: the goal itself can be destructive, and the price of success can be overridden by others. [2, «Качество онтологии»]

The limiting situation of the first volume is one way to determine the quality of our relationship with reality. It does not exhaust the advanced model of volume two. It is also necessary to explore the everyday ability to learn, maintain communication, accept consequences, distinguish between scales and remain open to the unknown.

### The Scenario Book

The Scenario Book connects Dynamic Iconic Space, the Operating System and subjecthood in a concrete situation. It describes the rules by which a recognized event is linked to expectations, available operations and possible responses. It does not determine the act in advance. [3, разд. II, §22а]

The possible, available and enacted responses are distinguished here. A possibility may exist and go unrecognized. A recognized option may be practically unavailable for lack of a skill or resource. Even with all the necessary means, the act may still never take place.

For example, an employee may understand the need to stop a wrong decision but not know the procedure for appealing; know it but have no access to the decision maker; have access and still refuse action. These provisions require different explanations. Each of these cannot be reduced to lack of courage or moral maturity.

## 7 Society and Modernization

The first one is about direct responsibility in the space of specific relationships. The second is the responsibility mediated by social functions, roles and institutions. Numeration does not set the scale of human worth. Direct intimacy is capable of supporting care but also pressure; the institute is able to protect a stranger but also conceal responsibility for the procedure. [2, «Модернизация»; 3, разд. IV]

Moving to a social function requires distinguishing between person and position, personal sympathy and duty, belonging and competence. The judge must hear the case of a stranger regardless of his personal situation. However, the performance of a function should not eliminate the visibility of a particular person affected by the decision.

Derived ontologies arise in specialized fields such as law, science, education, administration and technology. Each makes certain features of a situation visible while potentially leaving others outside its scope. Interontological translation recovers the conditions that give a description its meaning. Economic, legal and pastoral accounts of one situation may bring different obligations into view. [3, разд. V]

Modernization in ICT is considered through the encounter of complex social functions and the development of human capacity to take them into account. The establishment of an institution does not yet mean that you have mastered the appropriate way. Legal proceedings can exist formally, whereas real decisions are still determined by personal loyalty. [2, «Модернизация»]

The horizontal axis of modernization concerns the transition between scales and ontologies; vertical  quality and distribution of subjecthood; eschatological  relationship of created form to its own boundary. An institution should not become the ultimate measure of man. [3, разд. IV, §54]

The first volume examines relationships between people through the distinction of closed and founded relation. The question is whether the participants can keep their own answer if one of them gives himself a final ground for the other. The Christian perspective links the possibility of complicity to a common ground that does not belong to any participant. The very proclamation of such a foundation does not guarantee the proper arrangement of relations: an institution can refer to God and at the same time replace human authorship. [1, «Опыт прикладной метафизики субъекта», гл. 4]

Collective subjecthood requires exploring how distributed recognition becomes a shared decision, who can correct it and who pays for the consequences. The commonwealth doesn't just come about because the leadership speaks for the team. Ontoxic ability to pay and credit link the demand for action with a real ability to accept its price and earn trust. These concepts do not amount to money: time, competence, obligations and conditions for reproducing joint action are also important. [3, разд. VI– VIII]

### The Common, Legitimacy and Cost

The reality of the general, the validity of its representation and the right to make a specific claim are subject to independent verification. The power to make decisions, the ability to correct them and the responsibility to bear consequences may be different people's. So the study determines who identified the options available, who could object and who was actually priced. [3, разд. VII–VIII]

Onto-credit capacity expresses a reasonable expectation that promise, action and responsibility will remain linked. Payable ability is the ability to accept a valid price for an answer. Onto-capital describes the accumulated opportunities for credible initiative and cooperation, while onto-budget shows how they are produced, spent, and reproduced. [3, разд. VII]

## 8 How CTM Analysis Works

### Reconstructing the Actual Configuration

The CTM analysis explores the architecture of the answer, not satisfies itself with an object's self-description. First, you have to limit the case: what organization, practice or solution is being investigated, over what period, on what materials and in relation to what function. Then the actual conditions, the participants' map, the available operations and the distribution of subjecthood are reconstructed. [3, разд. X]

The statement and the action are then compared. Who notices a mistake? Could his message change the decision? Who has the right to stop this? Is it appropriate for the authorities to act with responsibility for consequences? The answers should be based on documents, observations and comparable evidence, not just participant declarations.

Subject topography shows the carriers and places of concentration of action possibilities. Topology considers the relationships between them: how initiative, objection or responsibility passes from one participant to another. Vector analysis adds direction of change over time. Increased activity or mobilization may be accompanied by reduced ability to object and correct. [3, разд. VIII–IX]

### The Vector of Subjecthood

A vector profile describes the bearer, level and direction of change, the distribution of powers, the dynamics, correspondence with the stated function and solvency. The analysis takes time and scale into account. Centralization, for example, may temporarily restore the capacity to act and then entrench the removal of initiative. A conclusion must refer to an established configuration of participants, time and level. Otherwise, evidence from different configurations may suggest a spurious causal link. [3, разд. IX–X]

### Modes of Correspondence

CTM distinguishes five modes of relation between the stated function and the function actually produced. In the authentic mode, the function is substantially fulfilled. In the projective mode, a new architecture of possibilities is created. In the compensatory mode, one capacity makes up for or masks a shortfall in another. In the simulative mode, the outward signs of the function persist without its corresponding substance. The inversion mode produces the opposite of the stated effect. [3, §105]

Compensation is not necessarily harmful: temporary emergency mobilization can support the work of a weak institution. The question is its price, duration and consequences. The inversion should also not be an accusative label: it must show which connections produce the opposite result.

### Verification and Suspending an Inference

The information indicates that the map, model or operating conditions are inadequate. The signal also requires interpretation: an unusual result can have several causes. The analysis should consider alternative reconstruction and indicate which evidence would change the conclusion. [3, разд. II и X]

The fail-closed principle prohibits the continuation of justification or application of a scenario as if necessary conditions were preserved when this is not shown after the relevant inconsistency has occurred. It does not mean an automatic ban on any activity in any uncertainty. Its function  is to prevent the unproven transition from being passed over by an established one.

The investigation may end in uncertainty. Lack of sufficient proof is not the same as evidence of absence. Analytical traceability requires keeping the path from material to conclusion, and inter-analytic testing  comparing reconstructions by different researchers. [3, разд. X–XII]

## 9 Design and Practical Application

The positive horizon of CTM is given by Christian Revelation: the project seeks to express and make practically acquired human understanding revealed in Christ. A specific organizational decision does not have the status of a gospel ordinance. Theological foundations and the structure of the institution require independent analysis and design work.

The CTM is a transition from diagnostics to design. Once the limitation is identified, you can change how information is exchanged, how powers are distributed, how participants are trained and how consequences are taken. But the project has to be based on specific resources and connections, and its results are subject to verification. [3, разд. XI]

An example: an organization declares that employees have the right to report errors. The messages are accepted, but the manager considers them disloyal and there is no procedure to review decisions. It's not enough to add another form of feedback. It is necessary to find out who is examining the message, whether that participant has the right to change the decision and what consequences it has for the applicant.

A possible project includes defining review powers, protecting honest reporting, teaching fact-checking and clearly allocating responsibility. It's checked to see if the valid messages have influenced the actions. The number of forms filled out does not prove the result. This is an example of using the difference in CT, not a report on an experiment that's already been done.

In the original philosophical statement, the question "I am" is revealed by examining who a person claims to be. A certain claim is tested: for example, whether he actually acts as the author of a decision or merely attributes the finished result to himself. The question of the criteria for verification itself arises. It cannot be eliminated by a single procedure: the procedure also involves grounds that must be available for consideration. [1, «К онтологии Логоса»]

In personal practice, you can start with a specific episode: what happened; what was recognized as possible; what precondition ruled out other answers; what means actually existed; what was done. Then a certain judgment is tested, like: I couldn't refuse. The discovery of the possibility of refusal does not prove that refusal would be correct or safe. It allows us to examine the basis of our own decision more accurately.

Design of conditions for freedom has a limit: you can pass on experiences, open access to information and create objection, but you cannot guarantee that another person will use them in a certain way. Otherwise, the formation of a subject becomes management by a predetermined outcome.

## 10 The Church and the Actualization of Tradition

The definition of the Church as an infrastructure for personal eschatology poses a question about how church life helps one become capable of one's own answer to God. The infrastructure includes stored experience, worship, mystery, learning, communication and responsibility. Functional consideration is carried out in the Church's theological understanding and does not exhaust its secrets. [1, «Функционал Церкви»; 3, разд. XIII]

In this perspective, it is important to distinguish between the presence of a church form and its actualization in human life. The number of participants, discipline or administrative resilience indicate certain aspects of the activity, but they themselves do not attest to the completeness of the Christian response or act of grace.

The author's distinction between actualization and reforming emphasizes the need to master tradition. The lack of understanding of the purpose of practice is not yet a reason to eliminate it. However, the historical preservation of the form also does not exempt the question of how it works now. ### Autonomy and Non-sovereign Authorship

The first substantive question concerns the relationship between the radical demand for autonomy in the first volume and the genesis of authorship in the second. The distinction between self-sufficiency and independence explains a lot, but the need to define precisely the status of the requirement for an actual attempt at full self-reliance remains. ### Normative and Analytical Uses of Subjecthood

In the first volume, subjecthood is closely linked to the faithfulness of Reality and Logos. The methodological sections also examine real beginnings that may be erroneous or destructive. It is necessary to distinguish between the authenticity of authorship and the quality of its orientation. This distinction is particularly important when analyzing non-Christian institutions. The Christian basis of the method defines its boundary horizon, but the specific competencies and real capabilities of another subject must be described without their prior elimination due to confession differences.

### Ontic Reproduction and New Beginnings

A radical description of ontological action as a reproduction of distortion requires consensus with the need for sustainable skills and institutions. Repetition can preserve a lie, or it can maintain the conditions for responsible action. It is useful to clarify what exactly is reproduced and whether the possibility of revision remains. It's a substantive task of harmonizing the body, not just stylistic softening.

### Philosophical Argument and Christian Confession

The recognition of an independent Reality, the impossibility of self-reliance and the necessity for responsibility should not be confused with the proof of all Christian theology. The project combines philosophical argumentation, interpretation of experience and a confessional basis. Each transition requires its own preconditions.

The question of the uniqueness of the Christian answer is particularly important. It should be established which alternatives are considered, on what criteria they are excluded and whether the criteria themselves do not imply a conclusion already reached. It is not a requirement to give up a confession; it is a requirement to distinguish its content from the evidence of a particular argument.

Natural-scientific comparisons, numerical illustrations and cultural generalizations of the first volume should be evaluated separately from philosophical differences. ## Conclusion

The CTM examines how a person who has been given the existence and ability to act becomes the author of his own answer. Philosophical presentation reveals the relationship to Reality and the limits of self-reasoning; theological  the giftedness of freedom and its Christian horizon; methodological  specific conditions for recognition, action, responsibility and correction.

The practical application begins with a specific question about one's own life or how to act together. What's recognized as real here? Why? What are the possibilities? Who has the right to start and fix it? Who's taking the consequences? The answers must be verified and the proposed changes  are relevant to the actual conditions.

In the church perspective, this work is aimed at developing and updating the experience. The church helps a person to acquire the ability to answer to God, but it does not eliminate his authorship. Analysis may detect obstacles and inconsistencies, but it does not replace the answer itself or confirm the final effect of grace. Keeping this boundary is a condition of applying the methodology itself.
