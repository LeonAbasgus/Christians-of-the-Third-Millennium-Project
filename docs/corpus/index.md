# CTM Corpus

The **primary source corpus of CTM I–III is Russian**. Translations are important access versions, but they do not override the Russian source when a terminological conflict occurs.

## CTM I

**Applied Metaphysics of the Subject** — the first stage of the project and the historical designation that must not be retrospectively replaced by *Applied Metaphysics of Subjecthood*.

Project repository: [Christians-of-the-Third-Millennium-Project](https://github.com/LeonAbasgus/Christians-of-the-Third-Millennium-Project)

Stable publication DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.19776054](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19776054)

## CTM II

The second volume develops the **genesis, ontogenesis, architecture, historical realization, projectuality, preservation, loss, and eschatological orientation of subjecthood**.

Official repository: [Christians-of-the-Third-Millennium-II](https://github.com/LeonAbasgus/Christians-of-the-Third-Millennium-II)

## CTM III

CTM III develops the reproducible methodology of **CTM Analysis**.

> **CTM analysis is the vector analysis of the level, direction, bearer, and distribution of subjecthood.**

Official repository: [Christians-of-the-Third-Millennium-III](https://github.com/LeonAbasgus/Christians-of-the-Third-Millennium-III)

Stable publication DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.22819373](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22819373)

## Current interpretive authority

For current methodological use, later explicit formulations of CTM III govern where they refine earlier formulations. Earlier formulations remain part of the historical genealogy of the concepts.

The **Canonical Meta-Document v1.1** records this relation without retroactively rewriting CTM I–II.

## Meta-Document and corpus

The **English Canonical Meta-Document v1.1** is the original of the present meta-level description. Its Russian, German, French, Greek, and Serbian versions are authorized translations. This does **not** alter the fact that the primary source corpus of CTM I–III is Russian.
