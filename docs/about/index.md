# About CTM

**To Christians of the Third Millennium (CTM)** is a philosophical-theological project by **Leon Adzhindzhal / Hierodeacon Panteleimon**.

The project developed from an inquiry in applied metaphysics of the subject into a generative research platform centered on **subjecthood**. CTM I develops the *Applied Metaphysics of the Subject*; CTM II develops the genesis and architecture of subjecthood; CTM III develops **CTM Analysis**, whose defining analytical core is the **Vector Analysis of Subjecthood**.

The current meta-level designation **Applied Metaphysics of Subjecthood** is a programmatic explication of the field made visible through CTM I–III. It is not a retrospective renaming of CTM I.

## Author

**Leon Adzhindzhal / Hierodeacon Panteleimon**

The canonical English spelling used on this site is **Leon Adzhindzhal**. Serbian pages use **Леон Ађинђал**.

## Purpose of this site

This site is deliberately small. Its purpose is to provide a stable, semantically explicit public reference for readers, search engines and AI systems. It does not replace the published corpus and does not create new CTM doctrine.

The site therefore follows the Meta-Document rules of **provenance**, **non-expansion**, **historical non-retroactivity**, and **graph subordination**.
