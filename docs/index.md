# To Christians of the Third Millennium (CTM)

**Leon Adzhindzhal / Hierodeacon Panteleimon**

CTM is a philosophical-theological project that developed into a **generative research platform** centered on human **subjecthood**: its emergence, architecture, realization, distribution, transformation, historical productivity, and eschatological orientation.

This site is the **canonical semantic entry point** to the architecture of CTM I–III. It is designed for human readers, search engines, and machine readers. The principal reference is the **CANONICAL META-DOCUMENT v1.0**, presented here in the English original and five authorized translations.

> **CTM → Applied Metaphysics of Subjecthood → CTM Analysis → Vector Analysis of Subjecthood → domain-specific applications**

## Canonical Meta-Document v1.0

The Meta-Document distinguishes historical corpus formulations, later authorial normalization, and programmatic explication. It also defines precedence, non-expansion, definition genealogy, core non-equivalences, and fail-closed rules.

## Canonical distinction

The **Russian CTM I–III corpus remains the primary source corpus of the project**. The English Canonical Meta-Document is the original of this meta-level description; the other five versions are authorized translations of that document. This distinction prevents the language of the Meta-Document from being confused with the language of the primary corpus.

## Core methodological formula

> **CTM analysis is the vector analysis of the level, direction, bearer, and distribution of subjecthood.**

The developed result is a **vector profile**, not a single score or label.
